# Customer Segmentation Visualization & Advanced Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer behavior and churn patterns in a telecommunications dataset as part of my internship with **Saiket Systems**.

The objective is to explore customer segments, understand how tenure and monthly charges vary across groups, identify patterns associated with customer churn, and derive actionable business insights that may help improve customer retention.

## Objectives

- Understand and prepare the dataset for analysis.
- Perform data cleaning and exploratory data analysis (EDA).
- Segment customers according to tenure.
- Compare average monthly charges across customer segments.
- Analyze churn patterns across demographics, payment methods, internet services, and contract types.
- Present findings through visualizations and business-oriented recommendations.

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas and NumPy
- Matplotlib and Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook
- Git and GitHub

## Project Workflow

1. **Dataset Exploration:** Inspect records, column data types, and missing values.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Handle missing values, check duplicate records, and standardize column names.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Examine summary statistics, numerical distributions, and churn proportions.
4. **Customer Segmentation:** Group customers into tenure categories and compare their characteristics.
5. **Advanced Analysis:** Explore churn patterns by tenure, demographics, payment methods, and contract types.
6. **Business Insights:** Translate observed patterns into recommendations for further investigation and customer retention.

## Key Areas of Analysis

- Customer tenure groups: 0–12 months, 13–36 months, and 37+ months
- Average monthly and total charges across tenure groups
- Churn rates across customer segments
- Churn by contract type and payment method
- Churn by gender and senior-citizen status
- Internet service and customer lifecycle patterns

## Key Findings

The analysis observed higher churn among customers with shorter tenure and customers on month-to-month contracts. Electronic-check users and customers using fiber-optic internet also showed higher churn rates in this dataset.

These are observed associations, not proof of causation. Further investigation is needed to understand the factors behind these patterns.
